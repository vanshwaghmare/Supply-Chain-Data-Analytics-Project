# Supply Chain Data Analytics Project

## 1. Project Overview

Supply chain management plays an important role in business operations, ensuring that products move efficiently from suppliers to customers. It involves multiple activities such as order processing, inventory management, warehouse operations, supplier coordination, and product delivery.

This project focuses on analyzing supply chain operations to understand business performance, identify operational challenges, and generate meaningful insights from raw data. The analysis covers different aspects of supply chain management, including customer and order performance, inventory levels, product categories, supplier performance, and shipment risks.

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics approach, starting with raw data processing and cleaning using Python, followed by data storage and analysis using MySQL, and finally connecting the data to Microsoft Power BI to develop interactive dashboards.

The main objective is to transform raw supply chain data into meaningful business insights that can help understand operational performance, identify potential risks, and support data-driven decision-making.

## 3. Project Workflow

The project follows a structured end-to-end data analytics workflow:

1. **Data Loading:** Loaded raw supply chain datasets into Google Colab using Python and Pandas.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Identified and handled missing values, duplicate records, inconsistent data types, and other data quality issues.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Explored datasets to understand data distributions, identify patterns, analyze trends, and generate initial insights.
4. **Data Export:** Exported the cleaned and processed datasets into CSV format for further analysis.
5. **SQL Integration:** Imported cleaned datasets into MySQL for structured data storage and SQL-based analysis.
6. **Power BI Integration:** Connected the data to Power BI and performed data modeling, data transformation, and other BI operations.
7. **Dashboard Development:** Developed interactive dashboards to visualize supply chain performance and analyze important business metrics.

## 4. Power BI Dashboard Pages

The Power BI dashboard consists of six analytical pages, each focusing on a different area of supply chain operations.

### 1. Supply Chain Overview

Provides an overall view of supply chain operations through key performance indicators (KPIs) and business metrics to understand overall performance.

### 2. Customer and Order Analysis

Focuses on customer behavior and order performance to identify customer-related patterns and understand order trends.

### 3. Inventory and Warehouse Analysis

Analyzes inventory levels and warehouse operations to understand stock management and inventory-related performance.

### 4. Product and Category Analysis

Provides insights into product performance and category-wise trends to understand how different products and categories contribute to business operations.

### 5. Shipment Risk Analysis

Analyzes shipment performance, delivery delays, risk categories, and potential shipping issues to identify areas that may require operational attention.

### 6. Supplier Performance

Evaluates supplier-related performance to understand supplier contributions and identify potential areas for improving supply chain efficiency.
